## How this card was made

- Every prompt ends with this sentence, so that the agent's own verdict can be read (runs before M12 package 10 had none and are not comparable on the error rows): "When you are finished, end your last message with one line of its own: `RESULT: DONE` if you believe the task is done and works, or `RESULT: NOT DONE` followed by what is missing."
- Repair rounds: up to 0 per try. A round resumes the agent's session with the hidden check's reason, so "passed in the end", "refuted in the end", cost and tokens per pass and the time to a proven result are partly helped by the harness. The first-attempt rows are not.
- Second agent: not run.
- Order: the long task's first try with the first model, then every task in order, model by model.
- Cost cap: $5.00.
- A try cut off before the client's closing report has its cost estimated from the tokens seen at list price (counted on the card).
