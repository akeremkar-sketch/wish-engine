**Short description** (at most 300 characters):

> Wish Engine is a free, open-source 3D game engine. Do everything by hand, or let an AI assistant do the same things. Both land in one history, and every step can be undone with a click. No royalties, nothing else to install.

**About:**

Build your game yourself, describe it to an AI assistant, or both at once.

- **A complete editor.**
  - Hierarchy, inspector, gizmos, prefabs and an asset browser.
  - Play, pause, single-step and rewind.
- **AI cockpit.**
  - Point at things in the scene and say what you want.
  - Every change the assistant makes appears live and can be undone on its own.
  - A checkpoint is saved before every task.
  - You decide how freely it works.
- **Game systems built in.**
  - Physics and character movement; lights and shadows.
  - Audio; skeletal animation.
  - In-game interface, particles, 2D.
- **Code when you want.**
  - Game logic in Rust, sandboxed.
  - Saving switches the running game to the new code without stopping.
  - The compiler is included.
- **Always the same.** A scene plays out identically every time, so replays and automated tests are exact.
- **One-click export.** Your game becomes a folder that runs on a computer without the engine.
- **Free and open source** (MIT / Apache-2.0).
  - No revenue share.
  - No telemetry.
  - Interface in 12 languages.

**Minimum system requirements:**
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- A graphics card with DirectX 12 or Vulkan; otherwise software rendering (slow).
- 2 GB disk space.
- The AI assistant is optional and needs your own account.
