Dışa aktarılan oyunlara motorun lisans metinleri (`LICENSES.txt`) kendiliğinden eklenir; bunları oyununuzla birlikte dağıtmanız yeterlidir.
3. **Garanti yoktur.** Motor "olduğu gibi" sunulur; açık kaynak lisanslarının garanti ve sorumluluk sınırlamaları geçerlidir. Çalışmalarınızın yedeğini almanızı öneririz (motorun denetim noktaları yedeğin yerini tutmaz).

2. **Your games are yours.** The games, scenes, code and assets you make with Wish Engine belong to you. There is no royalty, commission or revenue share. Exported games include the engine's license texts (`LICENSES.txt`) automatically.
3. **No warranty.** The engine is provided "as is", under the warranty disclaimers and limitations of liability of its open-source licenses. Keep backups of your work.
4. **Privacy.**
   - The engine collects and sends no usage data (no telemetry).
   - If an unexpected error happens, a crash report is written on your computer. It is never sent anywhere by itself; you attach it yourself if you want to.
   - Editor preferences (language, recent projects) stay on your computer.
5. **AI.**
   - The engine connects to no AI service unless you connect one.
   - If you connect an assistant (such as Claude Code), that service's own terms and privacy policy apply, and any fees or quotas are between you and it.
   - The assistant works only in your open project, in the permission mode you choose.
