## 1. The game

**Ridgewatch** (`examples/ridgewatch`, a 2D project, the "Online party"
template): a storm has buried a mountain radio observatory. Its keepers must
repair five machines (the generator, the radio, the antenna, the telescope
and the boiler) before the storm cuts them off. One of them (two from seven
players) is **static**: a keeper in name only, who freezes the others where
they stand and drowns the station's lights. Anyone standing can ring the bell
in the hall, or call everyone over a frozen keeper's statue: a meeting, with
quick chat phrases and a vote; the most votes send a keeper into the storm.

- Keepers win when the five repairs are done, or when every static is out.
- The statics win when as many statics stand as keepers.
- The frozen and the voted out become ghosts: they drift through walls,
  can still repair, and see (and are seen by) the other ghosts only.
- A blackout shrinks every keeper's sight to 2.5 m on their own machine;
  the static sees as before. Holding `use` at the fuse box brings the lights
  back.

Controls (`input.json`): `move` (WASD or arrows), `use` (E or Space: ready in
the lobby, repair, freeze, vote), `bell` (R), `sabotage` (Q, the static),
`say_1` to `say_4` (1 to 4: "I was repairing a machine", "I suspect X", "X
was with me", "Let's skip this one", X being the keeper the meeting's cursor
is on). The numbers of the game are the `Rules` resource in the scene, so a
person tunes them in the inspector.

Alone (no online session) the game is a practice round: one keeper, the five
machines.

## 2. How it is made

Only with the engine's tools:

- `wen new examples/ridgewatch --kind 2d`;
- pictures: `tools/ridgewatch_art.py` (friction F24: `asset_create` makes
  one-shape pictures, a walk cycle needs frames that differ); the tileset and
  the two sprite sheets and four sounds with `asset_create`;
- the station: one `tilemap_edit` call (snow, nine rooms, walls, twelve
  doorways), 44 x 30 cells of 1 m;
- the scene: one `edit_apply` (the map, the bell, ten keepers, ten ghosts,
  ten private records, six machines, four doors, nine lamps, the eye, the
  interface) and `apply_look plain`;
- `wish.json` `net` (now `project_settings`, friction F22): the replicated
  components, `min_players` 4, `max_players` 10, the roles `keeper` and
  `static` (`count` 1, `more_from` [7]);
- the game code `scripts/src/lib.rs` (twelve systems), `wen build`;
- the tests, `wen test`.

**Who runs what.** The host's machine runs the match (`local.host`, or alone:
`Lobby.phase == Offline`, which a player's machine never says: F13). Every
machine runs the same code; the systems that change the match return at once
elsewhere. A player's machine shows what the host sends it and runs only its
own hands (the lobby's ready, the meeting's cursor, votes and chat as network
messages) and its own view (the camera, its `Vision2D`, the lamps).

**Hidden information.**

| What | How it stays hidden |
|---|---|
| Roles | the lobby deals them; each machine learns only its own (`LocalPlayer.role`); `MatchRoles` is the host's |
| The static's waits | `Private`, one entity per seat, `NetVisibility::Owner` |
| Who voted for whom | the host's `Ballots` resource (resources are never sent); `Station.voted` says only who has voted |
| Where the others are | `Replicated.players` written by the host every tick: a keeper is sent to a player who can see it (within that player's sight, which a blackout shortens, and `can_see` past walls and closed doors), to the ghosts, and to everyone at a meeting |
| Ghosts | `NetVisibility::Group` with the ghosts' bit, set only while one haunts |

**Doors** are entities with a `Collider` and an `Occluder`: near a keeper the
host makes the collider a sensor and the occluder off, so the door lets the
keeper through and lets sight through.

## 3. Tests

`examples/ridgewatch/tests` (`wen test`; in the workspace tests by
`wish_control/tests/script_tools.rs`,
`the_ridgewatch_party_game_is_played_by_bots_and_replays`, which also saves a
match hosted with `net` as a replay and plays it back):

| Test | Players | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `repairs` | 6 (5 bots) | roles on each machine (a keeper's never holds the static's), a machine has only its own private record, a golden picture of keeper 2's view (`keeper-view.png`), five keepers walk to the five machines and repair them: the keepers win, and every machine hears it |
| `vote` | 6 | the host rings the bell, every machine shows the meeting, cursors move by seat order, a chat line reaches another keeper through the host, five votes send the static out, the keepers win |
| `frozen` | 8 (two statics) | `more_from` deals two statics, the statics freeze four keepers, the blackout shrinks a keeper's sight and not the static's, a frozen keeper's machine has the other ghost and a standing keeper's none: the statics win |
| `boots` | alone | the practice round, the west door slides open |

The online ones play over a simulated network with 100 ms latency, 20 ms
jitter and 5 % loss, and each is then verified as its replay (the host's match
log played into a fresh host world gives the same hash at every tick). In a
debug build the four take about six minutes (eight machines a tick).
